Schrodinger Equation: 

$$
i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t} \Psi(\mathbf{r}, t) = \hat{H} \Psi(\mathbf{r}, t)
$$

This equation describes the time evolution of a quantum system, where $\Psi(\mathbf{r}, t)$ is the wave function, $\hat{H}$ is the Hamiltonian operator, and $i\hbar$ is the imaginary unit times the reduced Planck constant.
